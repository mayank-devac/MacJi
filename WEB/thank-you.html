Requires macOS 14.6 or later · Release notes

  1. 📦

    Open the download

    Open MacJi.dmg from Downloads and drag MacJi into Applications.

  2. 🛡️

    Allow it once

    MacJi isn't notarized, so macOS asks first. Open it, choose Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. ♿️

    Grant Accessibility

    In MacJi's Settings, click Grant Access… and drag MacJi into the list. The picker works even without it.

  4. 🚀

    Type :: anywhere

    Try ::rocket in Notes, or press ⌃⌥⌘Space for the picker.
