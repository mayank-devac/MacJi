## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added
- Inline panel: type `::` and a term in any app to insert an emoji at the caret.
- Emoji picker in the menu bar with search, categories, recents and keyboard navigation.
- Global shortcut for the picker (default ⌃⌥⌘Space); it opens at the caret when the icon is hidden.
- Settings: Accessibility status, shortcut, trigger, menu-bar icon, open at login, automatic updates.
- Sparkle updates signed with EdDSA.
